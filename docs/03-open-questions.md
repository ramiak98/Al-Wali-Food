# Open questions for Rami

The build can start without these (placeholders are in place), but each one must be
answered before launch. The first five matter most.

## Must have
1. **Product catalog.** The real list of products per category: name (English and Arabic),
   sizes, ingredients, allergens, nutrition panel, storage, UPC and case pack. A photo of
   each label (front and back) is enough to start.
2. **Subcategories.** Are the proposed subcategories in [01-sitemap.md](01-sitemap.md#subcategories) right? Add or remove.
3. **Logo and brand assets.** Logo files, any existing brand colors or fonts, packaging photos.
4. **Company details for the legal pages.** Legal entity name and type (LLC / Inc.), state of
   registration, mailing address, phone, and the emails that should receive forms
   (e.g. info@, sales@, privacy@).
5. **Domain.** Do you already own a domain (e.g. alwalifood.com)?

## Story and claims
6. **Brand story.** Founding year, the founder or family, and where the products are made
   (Lebanon? which region? your own facility or partner producers?).
7. **Certifications we can mention.** Halal, FDA facility registration, HACCP, ISO 22000,
   Non-GMO, kosher, organic. We'll only show what's certified.
8. **Where you're sold.** Which states/regions, and can we list stores publicly on a map,
   or should Where to Buy launch with the form only?
9. **Arabic brand name** spelling, if there is one on the packaging.

## Photos
10. Do you have your own photos from Lebanon (Baalbek, Bekaa, harvests), or should we use
    licensed stock for launch and plan a shoot later?

## Smaller choices (defaults chosen; tell us only if you disagree)
- Analytics: **Plausible** (privacy-friendly, no cookie banner needed for it).
- Newsletter sign-up: **included** in the footer, off by default in forms.
- Store finder: **map + list**, if stores can be listed.
- Language: **English** with Arabic product names; full Arabic version later.
- Social handles: Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn — please send the handles once created.
- Prop 65: please confirm whether any product carries a California Prop 65 warning.
