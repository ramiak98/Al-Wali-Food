# Events (built now, launched later)

The page and CMS model are built in the first release but **hidden from the navigation
and sitemap** until Rami publishes the first event.

**SEO title:** Events & Tastings | Al Wali Food
**Meta description:** Meet Al Wali Food at trade shows, in-store tastings and Lebanese food festivals across the US.

> **Heading:** Events & tastings
> **Subhead:** Meet us at trade shows, in-store tastings and Lebanese festivals.

**Layout:** Upcoming events (cards, soonest first) → Past events (smaller, with photos).

**Event fields (CMS):** title · type (Trade show / In-store tasting / Festival / Other) ·
start & end date/time · venue name · address · city/state · photo · description ·
external link (registration or booth number) · "For distributors" flag.

**Empty state** (if the page is opened with no upcoming events):
> No upcoming events right now. Follow us on Instagram to hear about our next tasting.

**Event detail page:** hero photo, date, location with map link, description, [Add to calendar] (.ics), [Get directions], and for trade shows: "Meeting us at the show? [Book a meeting]" → distributor form.
