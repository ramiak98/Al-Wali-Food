## Brand basics
- **Name:** Al Wali Food (Arabic: [confirm spelling, e.g. الوالي])
- **Tagline (proposed):** "The taste of Lebanon, from our family to yours." Alternatives: "Authentically Lebanese." · "Rooted in Lebanon."
- **Tone of voice:** warm, proud, generous, simple. Like a Lebanese host welcoming you to the table. Short sentences, sensory words (crunchy, golden, sun-ripened), occasional Arabic greetings (Ahlan wa sahlan, Sahtein) with the meaning clear from context. Avoid health or "best in the world" claims we can't prove.
