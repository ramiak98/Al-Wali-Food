This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("Al Wali Food," "we," "us")
collects, uses and shares personal information when you visit [www.alwalifood.com]
(the "Site") or contact us. It also explains your privacy rights.

## 1. Information we collect

**Information you give us**
- *Where-to-buy and general contact forms:* name, email address, ZIP code, products
  you're interested in, your favorite store, and your message.
- *Distributor and retailer inquiries:* name, company name, business type, business
  email, phone number, city and state, website, products of interest, volume estimates
  and your message.
- *Newsletter (if you opt in):* email address and your subscription preferences.

**Information collected automatically**
- Device and usage information, such as browser type, device type, pages viewed,
  referring page, approximate location derived from IP address, and date and time of
  visit, collected through cookies and similar technologies. See our
  [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies).
- If you click "Use my location" on the store finder, your browser shares your
  approximate location with us for that search only. We do not store it.

We do not collect sensitive personal information (such as financial account, health or
precise geolocation data) and we do not take payments on the Site.

## 2. How we use information
- To answer your questions and help you find our products.
- To respond to business inquiries and process order requests.
- To send newsletters or marketing emails if you asked to receive them.
- To operate, secure and improve the Site, prevent spam and fraud, and understand how
  visitors use it.
- To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

## 3. How we share information
We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only:
- **With service providers** that help us run the Site and our business, under contracts
  limiting their use of the data: website hosting ([Vercel]), content management
  ([Sanity]), form and email delivery ([Resend / Formspree]), spam protection
  ([Cloudflare Turnstile]), maps ([Google Maps / Mapbox]), analytics ([Plausible / Google
  Analytics]), newsletter ([provider]) and cookie consent ([Termly / CookieYes]).
- **With distributors or retailers**, only when needed to answer your request (for
  example, passing a store inquiry to the distributor serving your area). *[Confirm
  whether this happens.]*
- **For legal reasons**, to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect our
  rights, users or others.
- **In a business transfer**, such as a merger or sale of assets.

*If Google Analytics or advertising pixels (e.g. Meta, TikTok) are used, some state laws
treat that as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out as
described below.*

## 4. Your privacy choices
- **Cookies and analytics:** use the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer at any time.
- **Global Privacy Control (GPC):** we honor GPC browser signals as a request to opt out
  of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
- **Marketing emails:** click "unsubscribe" in any email, or contact us.

## 5. Your state privacy rights
Depending on where you live (including California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut,
Utah, Texas, Oregon and other states with privacy laws), you may have the right to:
- **Know / access** the personal information we have about you and how we use it;
- **Correct** inaccurate personal information;
- **Delete** personal information we collected from you;
- **Obtain a copy** in a portable format;
- **Opt out** of the sale or sharing of personal information, targeted advertising and
  profiling;
- **Not be discriminated against** for exercising these rights.

To make a request, email [privacy@alwalifood.com] or call [toll-free number, if
required] with the subject "Privacy Request." We will verify your identity by matching
information you provide with what we hold, and respond within 45 days (we may extend
this once by 45 days when reasonably necessary). You may use an authorized agent, who
must provide proof of your permission. If we deny your request, you may appeal by
replying to our decision; we will respond within the time required by your state's law.
