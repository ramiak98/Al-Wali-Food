## Our commitment
We aim to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**. These
guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people who are blind or
have low vision, are deaf or hard of hearing, or have motor, speech, cognitive or
learning disabilities.

## What we do
- Text and background colors meet WCAG contrast requirements.
- All images that carry meaning have text descriptions (alt text).
- The whole site can be used with a keyboard, with visible focus indicators and a
  "Skip to content" link.
- Pages use clear headings, landmarks and labels that work with screen readers.
- Forms have visible labels, clear error messages and do not rely on color alone.
- Animations respect your device's "reduce motion" setting.
- The site works at up to 200% zoom and on mobile devices.
- Nutrition facts shown as images also have a text version.
- We test with automated tools and manual checks, including screen readers
  ([VoiceOver / NVDA]), before launch and after major updates.

## Known limitations
- Some third-party content, such as embedded maps, may not be fully accessible. Every
  store on the map is also listed as text, and you can always contact us for help
  finding a store.
